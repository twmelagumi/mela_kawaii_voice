「めらめら出勤紀錄」（以下稱「本站」）是由粉絲志工個人營運的非官方粉絲網站，與 COVER 株式会社、hololive production 及熱千めら本人沒有任何關係。本頁說明本站會處理哪些資料、怎麼使用，以及你可以怎麼刪除。

1. 本站處理的資料

  • 使用 Google 登入時（周邊頁、音聲頁的「雲端同步」，可自由選擇是否使用）：Google 提供給登入服務的帳號識別碼、email、名稱與大頭貼網址，以及你標記的「已入手」紀錄（品項代號、數量、更新時間）。
  • 個人設定（可自由選擇是否填寫）：你設定的暱稱，以及你上傳的頭像圖片（上傳前會在你的瀏覽器裡裁成正方形並縮小）。
  • 沒有登入時：「已入手」紀錄、語言、深淺色等設定只存在你自己的瀏覽器（localStorage），不會傳送到本站。
  • 瀏覽統計：本站使用 GoatCounter 統計頁面瀏覽次數。GoatCounter 不使用 Cookie，也不保存可以辨識個人的資訊，只記錄瀏覽的頁面、來源網站、瀏覽器與裝置類型等統計資料。
  • 投稿與問題回報：透過 Google 表單送出投稿或回報時，你填寫的內容由 Google 表單保存，本站營運者會看到這些內容。

2. 使用目的

  • 在不同裝置之間同步你的「已入手」紀錄。
  • 統計每個周邊、音聲品項的入手人數（例如入手人數排行榜）。品項的入手人數只會顯示人數，不會顯示是誰。
  • 排行榜等公開頁面上，會顯示你設定的暱稱和頭像（沒有設定就不會顯示，或顯示為「匿名」）。不會公開你的 email 和 Google 帳號名稱。
  • 審核投稿、處理問題回報，以及了解網站的使用狀況。

本站不會把你的資料用在上述以外的用途，不會販售，不會用於廣告，也不會提供給第三方（法律要求的情況除外）。

3. 使用的外部服務

  • Supabase：登入、資料庫與檔案儲存（保存上面第 1 點的登入資料、已入手紀錄、暱稱與頭像）。
  • Google：Google 帳號登入、Google 表單、Google 試算表（網站內容的資料來源）。
  • GitHub Pages：網站主機。GoatCounter：瀏覽統計。
  • YouTube、X（Twitter）：網站內嵌的影片與貼文。播放或顯示這些內容時，這些服務可能依它們自己的政策使用 Cookie 等技術。

4. 保存期間與刪除

登入後保存的資料，會保存到你刪除為止。在周邊頁或音聲頁的「雲端」區按「刪除雲端紀錄」，就會刪除你的已入手紀錄、暱稱、頭像和登入帳號（你瀏覽器裡的紀錄不受影響）。暱稱和頭像也可以隨時在「個人設定」修改或移除。不當的頭像或暱稱，本站可以不經通知移除，並停用上傳功能。如果無法自行刪除，也可以透過下方的表單聯絡我們處理。

5. 安全

資料庫設定成每個人只能讀取和修改自己的紀錄。不過網路服務無法保證絕對安全，請不要在本站輸入不必要的個人資料。

6. 本政策的變更

本政策更新時，會修改本頁內容並更新上方的生效日期。

7. 聯絡方式

關於本政策或你的資料，請透過問題回報表單聯絡我們。

"Mela Mela Shift Log" ("this site") is an unofficial fan site run by a volunteer fan. It is not affiliated with COVER Corporation, hololive production, or Achichi Mela. This page explains what data this site handles, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

1. Data this site handles

  • If you sign in with Google (the optional "Cloud" sync on the Merch and Voice pages): the account identifier, email address, name and profile picture URL that Google provides to the sign-in service, and the "owned" items you mark (item code, count and time updated).
  • Profile settings (optional): the nickname you set and the avatar image you upload (cropped to a square and shrunk in your browser before upload).
  • If you don't sign in: your "owned" list and settings such as language and theme are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and are never sent to this site.
  • Analytics: page views are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and stores no personally identifiable information — only statistics such as the page viewed, the referring site, and the browser and device type.
  • Submissions and bug reports: anything you send through a Google Form is stored by Google Forms and read by the site maintainer.

2. How the data is used

  • To sync your "owned" list across your devices.
  • To count how many people own each merch or voice item (for example, a ranking). Item counts only show the number of people, never who they are.
  • To show the nickname and avatar you set on public pages such as rankings (if you haven't set them, nothing is shown or you appear as "Anonymous"). Your email address and Google account name are never made public.
  • To review submissions, handle bug reports and understand how the site is used.

Your data is not used for anything else, is never sold or used for advertising, and is not shared with third parties except where required by law.

3. Services this site uses

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and file storage (stores the sign-in data, "owned" list, nickname and avatar in section 1).
  • Google: Google sign-in, Google Forms, and Google Sheets (the source of the site's data).
  • GitHub Pages: hosting. GoatCounter: analytics.
  • YouTube and X (Twitter): embedded videos and posts. When they load or play, these services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

4. Retention and deletion

Data saved after you sign in is kept until you delete it. Press "Delete cloud data" in the "Cloud" section of the Merch or Voice page to delete your "owned" list, nickname, avatar and sign-in account (the list in your browser is not affected). You can change or remove your nickname and avatar any time in "Profile settings". Inappropriate avatars or nicknames may be removed without notice and uploading disabled. If you can't do this yourself, contact us using the form below.

5. Security

The database is set up so that each person can only read and change their own records. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please don't enter personal information you don't need to.

When this policy changes, this page will be updated together with the effective date above.
